Sharebuff

One-shot encrypted drop for text and files. Decryption happens in your browser; the server never sees the data or the PIN.

⚠️ This link carries no secret

You opened the bare Sharebuff address. A real share link continues after a # — that part stays in your browser and is never sent to any server. Ask the sender for the complete URL (and the PIN, ideally over a different channel).

Retrieving with the correct PIN permanently destroys the secret on the server. A wrong PIN does not (10 attempts allowed, with a short cooldown after each miss).
